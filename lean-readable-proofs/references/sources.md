# Source evidence

Use this as a reading map, not a universal ranking or a verified human-only corpus. The evidence consists of representative author-attributed proof source in ten human-led projects. Historical snapshots were used where useful; author names and dates cannot certify absence of AI assistance. The survey did not rebuild the repositories or audit all transitive axioms.

Follow each repository's license and attribution requirements when copying substantial code. The short excerpts in patterns.md are teaching examples from the linked contexts.

Important limits:
- Mathematics in Lean contains intentional exercise holes. Use its completed examples.
- The historical FLT snapshot leaves major results such as Mazur_Frey and Wiles_Frey unfinished. Its elementary completed lemmas do not establish completion of FLT.
- Carleson's current documentation distinguishes its completed continuous theorem from a sorry-dependent Carleson–Hunt development. Local completed bodies are not a whole-project audit.
- Today's physlib explicitly includes AI-generated contributions in PhyslibAlpha. The selected examples come from an earlier author-attributed PhysLean snapshot.
- Liquid's architecture transfers to Lean 4, but its Lean 3 syntax does not transfer unchanged.
- Even strong repositories contain opaque names or cumbersome proofs. Apply the underlying lesson selectively.
